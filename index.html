Official Invitation to Participate in ICL Season-13

The Indian Combat League (ICL) is proud to announce the 13th Season of the Indian Combat League, scheduled to be held on 17.11.2026 at the Kolhapur Divisional Sport's Complex, as a prestigious District Level Championship.

Tournament Vision

"ICL Season-13 is envisioned as a high-standard competitive platform for Kids, Junior, Seniors and Debutant Fighters & Amateur MMA athletes elite tournament fighters in promoting discipline, sportsmanship, safety, and professional growth in the field of Mixed Martial Arts (MMA) in the Nation."

Addressed To Danish Khan (Coach) Combat Fighters, Mira Road
Authorized Signatory R. K. Bharat Chief Executive Officer (CEO), ICL

Governing Affiliations

As officially printed on the sanctioned ICL notification:

  • • National Martial Arts Committee - India: Primary Governing Authority under whose aegis the championship is held.
  • • Asian Martial Arts Games Committee: Official Affiliation.
  • • World Martial Arts Games Committee: Official Affiliation.
  • • TAFISA Member: Recognized by the International Olympic Committee (IOC), WHO, ICSSPE, and UNESCO.
