Indian Combat League (ICL Season-13)

The Indian Combat League operates under the aegis of the National Martial Arts Committee - India, affiliated with the Asian & World Martial Arts Games Committee, and member of TAFISA (recognized by IOC, WHO, ICSSPE, and UNESCO).
